## Muse Drive crash (found 2026-11-21)
FATAL EXCEPTION on launch, twice, same stack: ClassNotFoundException androidx.collection.ArrayMap, thrown from DriveMediaService.<init> (DriveMediaService.java:55). The APK is missing the androidx.collection runtime dependency its media/car libraries reference. Fix: add androidx.collection:collection to the app dependencies (or audit the dependency graph for what strips it), rebuild, retest on device.
